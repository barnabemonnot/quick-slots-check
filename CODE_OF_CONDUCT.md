# Code of conduct

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise claims and code, not people.
- Naming an affected system is fine; shaming its team is not. State the mechanism, the evidence and the fix.
- No harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or close contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

Report conduct problems to the maintainers through a private security advisory, marked "conduct".
The maintainer, Barnabé Monnot (@barnabemonnot), handles conduct reports.
